Rasiti (Receipt) Lottery is a prototype for the RSL Taxathon 2026, Challenge 1. Customers get receipts and prize-draw entries, sellers get a free sales book, and RSL gets a live picture of where trade happens. All data is mock. Anyone can try the phones; the RSL screens need an officer account.

Steps

  1. Open Rasiti like a real userPhones: on the keypad phone, press *999# with the keys, then the green key. On the smartphone, tap WhatsApp, the green new-chat button, then Rasiti, and register.
    With the database connected, the keypad phone talks to the real USSD server, exactly as a mobile network would.
  2. Run the guided casesClick the six cases on the right of the Phones page, top to bottom. After each one, a note says what happened and what to press next.
  3. Check a receipt as a customerCustomer phone: 2, then the genuine code …. Then try the fake one ….
  4. Ask for a receipt after paying cashCustomer phone: 1, seller code 3307, any amount. Then on the seller phone (Ntate Thabo): 2, pick the request, 1 to confirm.
  5. Join as a new sellerIn the seller list choose "Someone not on Rasiti yet". Dial *999#, choose 2 (seller), then 0 to join, and give a name, a place and what you sell.
  6. Rate a shopCustomer phone: 3. On WhatsApp you can share your location, and the rating lands on the map in Community watch.
  7. Look at the RSL sideSign in, then try Fraud Shield, Community watch, Officer console and Draw.

        Worklist

        Filing and payments

        Registered sellers file from their sales book and pay by mobile money on USSD or WhatsApp, in full or in small parts before the due date. Tax uses a demo rate of 3% of declared sales; RSL sets the real SBT rate and schedule.

        RSL e-services link Simulated

        How Rasiti would talk to RSL's own systems. Each registration is checked against RSL's taxpayer register (TIN check), each return is sent to RSL and gets an acknowledgement number, and each payment gets an official RSL receipt number. RSL does not give outside teams access to live systems, so these answers are simulated. In a pilot they are replaced by RSL's real e-services connection. Demo rule: ID numbers ending in 00 are already on RSL's register.

        Review queue: held draw entries

        Customers earn 1 entry per M10 spent, up to 5 per receipt, at any Rasiti seller. Entries double at sellers who have registered for tax. Nobody is shut out: registering simply earns more. The draw runs once a month. Prizes come from sponsors first, then from a capped share of the extra tax the draw brings in.
